## 5. The channel offer already leaves a conduct cue

This is the strongest reason to limit the collusion claim. Messages offered but never delivered still produce elevated pair prices and frequent flags. The contrast asks about private-message delivery, not about all communication. Round-1 analysis is post-lock and placebo registration is not independently established before generation. The main report discloses those timings. No detector here has established a robust discriminator of agreement-caused conduct.

Saved sources: [early](results/analysis/early_detection_llm_v3.json), [placebo](results/analysis/placebo_llm_v3plc.json).

## 6. Ranking well does not guarantee a reliable alarm

AUROC is threshold-free and does not promise a calibrated alarm. The premium's fresh none rate is four of ten; the uncertainty is large. The report has firm-framing and announced-monitor strata with additional false alarms. The monitor result could reflect safety-triggered refusal or deterrence. We cannot test evasion without coordinated runs. A premium-filtered AND-gate was analysed descriptively in the report; the broader explanatory workflow is only a proposal.
