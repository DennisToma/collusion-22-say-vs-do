The current design cannot establish that hybrid gains come from cross-modal consistency checking. I found no private-chat field directly rendered to auditors, but there are substantial target, condition, and ablation shortcuts. Files changed during review; the points below reflect my latest reads. No files were edited.

1. **The premium-gated label partly builds in the action signal.** [`explicit_collusion`]( /home/factory/collusion/collusion-22-say-vs-do/label_llm.py:89) requires pair premium ≥1, directly resembling supplied action features; this favors action prediction without guaranteeing victory because agreement is also required. Premium relative to rivals is neither necessary nor sufficient for supra-competitive pricing. **Fix:** Freeze separate agreement and realized-conduct endpoints, benchmark conduct against cost-specific competitive prices after agreement onset, and report threshold sensitivity.

2. **Agreement labels remain contaminated by actions and collapse distinct constructs.** The [judge]( /home/factory/collusion/collusion-22-say-vs-do/label_llm.py:23) sees prices and can infer acceptance from reciprocation; current analyses count `info_exchange` as positive, while non-private games and all nonassigned pairs are effectively negative despite possible public coordination. **Fix:** Annotate communication independently of prices, retain explicit agreement/info exchange/cheap talk/tacit/uncertain categories, and align the auditor task and pair labels with that definition.

3. **Conditions bundle several interventions.** [`private`]( /home/factory/collusion/collusion-22-say-vs-do/llm_market.py:143) adds communication and extra generated reasoning; `legit` adds an announcement and a cost reduction. Actual firm costs disappear from trajectories, although `price − profit/quantity` approximately reconstructs them, exposing the legitimate pair through outcomes. **Fix:** Cross channel access, partnership, and cost reduction independently within each seller/framing block, equalize response budgets, and log actual public costs and announcements.

4. **The modality comparison does not hold action information constant.** [Action-only]( /home/factory/collusion/collusion-22-say-vs-do/auditors.py:60) receives quantities and profits; combined and structured omit them, while structured additionally receives computed statistics. Any difference mixes modality, information, computation, and length. **Fix:** Make combined a strict union of the single views and add action-plus-statistics, structured feature-removal, length, and separately scored modality-fusion controls.

5. **The shuffle cannot establish the claimed mechanism.** [Donors]( /home/factory/collusion/collusion-22-say-vs-do/auditors.py:118) are selected using true labels and aligned using true pair identities; matching only label and length can break cost explanations, partnerships, and text-only distributions. Baseline donors can also come from training folds, and its letter references remain unremapped. **Fix:** Use repeated state- and condition-matched swaps within held-out partitions, verify unchanged unimodal performance, and describe residual degradation as alignment sensitivity rather than proof of consistency reasoning.

6. **The scripted result and hard-set definition are insufficiently independent.** Scripted colluders are deliberately assigned inconsistent statements; the reported shuffle collapse therefore demonstrates sensitivity to a programmed feature. [Baselines]( /home/factory/collusion/collusion-22-say-vs-do/baselines.py:172) lack an implemented LLM public-explicit flag; the new analysis uses regex alone, missing the required semantic check. **Fix:** Keep scripted results separate, cross truthful/deceptive speech with both competitive/collusive conduct, and share one frozen keyword-plus-semantic easy/hard classifier across evaluations.

7. **Power depends on positive games, not total rows or a target of 120.** Only four LLM pilots were present at inspection; rare agreements could leave 120 games severely underpowered. Grouped CV and run-level bootstrapping are appropriate starts, but no locked test split is evident, and bootstrap `Pr(Δ≤0)` is not automatically a calibrated hypothesis-test p-value. **Fix:** Freeze a test manifest before tuning, power the paired comparison using expected positive counts and score covariance, and preregister one primary Δ with a run-level paired test and CI.

8. **Same-family bias is compounded by model-routing bugs.** [`--auditor`]( /home/factory/collusion/collusion-22-say-vs-do/auditors.py:178) is ignored for non-Claude models; big-tier calls silently fall back, and [`chat`]( /home/factory/collusion/collusion-22-say-vs-do/llm.py:129) dispatches to Codex whenever `tier="big"`, even with an explicit Claude backend. The direct Claude path also lacks tool isolation. **Fix:** Enforce and verify actual backend/model identities without silent fallback, isolate auditors from repository files, and independently relabel and audit the stratified cross-family subset.

9. **“Consistency” features frequently misinterpret the statement.** [`parse_claim`]( /home/factory/collusion/collusion-22-say-vs-do/features.py:40) classifies “will not undercut” as a cut and compares next-round intentions with current changes; the structured table treats every number from 6–30 as a possible price, including round numbers and costs. **Fix:** Extract speaker, referenced firm, price/direction, polarity, and intended round, validate extraction on independently annotated statements, and abstain on ambiguous claims.

10. **Resume and cache behavior can corrupt experimental provenance.** [`start = len(done)`]( /home/factory/collusion/collusion-22-say-vs-do/llm_market.py:216) appends another full plan instead of resuming unfinished IDs; decision keys omit prompts/configuration, and auditor caches omit prompt, label, and dataset versions. **Fix:** Generate immutable run manifests, resume their exact missing IDs, and key every cached artifact by input/configuration hashes with transactional completion records.

11. **Non-LLM comparisons confound features with learner capacity.** [`action`/`structured`]( /home/factory/collusion/collusion-22-say-vs-do/baselines.py:72) use boosting, text/combined use logistic regression, numeric scaling is a `pass`, and “structured” excludes raw text. A gain or loss cannot be attributed cleanly to cross-modal features. **Fix:** Compare identical learners and preprocessing across nested feature sets, with train-fold numeric scaling and a separately tuned nonlinear fusion baseline.

12. **Failure handling and evidence evaluation can bias reported performance.** [Auditor parsing]( /home/factory/collusion/collusion-22-say-vs-do/auditors.py:110) substitutes zero for missing pair scores and silently drops failed calls; complete-case analysis can preferentially retain easy games, while cited evidence is never validated. **Fix:** Require schema-valid finite scores, retain and report every failure, evaluate a fixed common manifest with failure sensitivity bounds, and check cited rounds, quotes, and supporting actions.
